Game data

The app handles a visible name, profile identifier, friendships, invitations, game state, moves, scores, deadlines and results. It does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS or financial data.

Advertising and providers

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banners. Google may process IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where applicable, the app shows Google’s consent form and privacy options. Game data is used to operate the service and is synchronized through infrastructure providers. We do not sell the game’s own data.

Automated-player training

After a deletion request, moves may be retained only as anonymized samples: board, move, score and date. The visible name, profile identifier, relationships and direct account link are removed. Samples may be used to train or evaluate an automated player.

Retention, security and rights

History is retained until deletion is requested or while needed to operate games. Production transmission uses HTTPS. You can request deletion in Settings and through the external deletion page. For access, correction or objection, contact CONTACT_EMAIL.

Changes

Material changes will be posted before taking effect. We will review this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration before changing AdMob, mediation or other SDKs.
